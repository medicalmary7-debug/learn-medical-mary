Terpenes 101

Terpenes are naturally occurring aromatic compounds found in many plants. Aroma and flavour are useful starting points, not proof of a health outcome.

  • They contribute to scent and flavour.
  • Different plants have different profiles.
  • Aroma alone does not establish a medical effect.

How to check for quality

Quality information helps people understand what they are looking at before they make assumptions.

  • Check for batch information and laboratory documentation.
  • Look for clear ingredients and testing information.
  • Be cautious of unclear labels or guaranteed results.

A slower kind of wellness

Holistic wellness can include small, realistic routines that leave room for personal context and professional guidance.

  • Notice what supports your general wellbeing.
  • Choose routines that are practical and sustainable.
  • Use qualified guidance for personal medical questions.
